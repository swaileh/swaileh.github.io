Research Areas

Research Philosophy

My research focuses on advancing the frontiers of artificial intelligence and machine learning, with particular emphasis on multimodal systems that can understand and process multiple types of data simultaneously.

I am particularly interested in developing AI systems that can bridge the gap between visual perception and natural language understanding, enabling machines to comprehend and reason about the world in ways that are more aligned with human cognition.

Primary Research Areas

Visual Question Answering

Developing AI systems capable of understanding images and answering natural language questions about their content. This involves combining computer vision and natural language processing to create more intuitive human-AI interfaces.

Computer Vision NLP Multimodal AI

Generative Diffusion Models

Researching state-of-the-art generative models for creating and manipulating visual content. These models have applications in image synthesis, editing, and creative AI tools.

Generative AI Deep Learning Image Synthesis

Pattern Recognition

Developing algorithms for recognizing complex patterns in structured and unstructured data. Applications include handwriting recognition, document analysis, and biometric identification systems.

Recognition Classification Deep Learning

Sequential Statistical Models

Machine learning of sequential statistical models for time series analysis, trajectory reconstruction, and sequence prediction tasks. This includes work on handwriting trajectory reconstruction and motion analysis.

Time Series LSTM TCN

Natural Language Processing

Computational approaches to understanding, interpreting, and generating human language. Research includes language modeling, information extraction, and text analysis from historical documents.

NLP Information Extraction Text Mining

Document Analysis & 3D Modeling

Advanced techniques for analyzing historical documents and creating 3D models of ancient maps and architectural plans. This work combines computer vision with digital humanities research.

Document Analysis 3D Modeling Digital Humanities
