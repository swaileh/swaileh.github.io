About Me

I am an accomplished Data Scientist with a PhD in Computer Science, specializing in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.

My extensive experience spans several cutting-edge domains, including:

  • Multimodal language models
  • Generative diffusion models
  • Trajectory reconstruction
  • Information extraction
  • Language modeling
  • Visual Question Answering

Throughout my career, I have held reputable research and academic positions at esteemed institutions such as the University of Rouen Normandy, INSA Rennes, CY Cergy Paris Université, and Huawei RTC Finland.
